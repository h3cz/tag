# Tag — standalone edition

A small, polished, open-source text chat app for people using their own provider or local models.

The first release installs reproducibly, opens without a backend account, streams and stops replies, keeps local conversations, and explains how keys/data are handled. Keys stay in tab memory; backups exclude credentials.

The separately hosted hecz.dev/chat retains managed models and account features. Public Tag is independent of its billing, database, integrations, and private configuration. Keep the core useful, portable, and easy to contribute to.
